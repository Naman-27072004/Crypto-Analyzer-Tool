Crypto Glossary & Quick Insights

24h Percentage Change

Measures the relative price change over the past 24 hours. Vital for identifying short-term momentum and volatility spikes.

Market Capitalization (MCap)

Calculated by multiplying the circulating coin supply by the current price. Indicates the relative size and stability of a token.

DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging)

An investment strategy where total funds are divided into equal periodic purchases to minimize the impact of volatility.

Slippage & Liquidity

The difference between expected trade price and execution price. Higher liquidity pools result in lower slippage.
